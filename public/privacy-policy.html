Information We Collect

1. Personal Information

Information that identifies you personally and/or can be used to contact you online or offline, such as your full name, physical address, email address, telephone number, photograph, profile picture, precise geo-location information (when you share restaurant locations or food posts), payment information (if applicable), and your username.

2. Non-Personal Information

Information that does not identify you personally and cannot be used to contact you directly. This includes your date of birth, age, ZIP code, city, gender, food preferences, favorite restaurants, as well as device-related data such as unique device ID, Internet Protocol (IP) address, hardware/software/firmware information. It may also include usage data such as browser type, app usage patterns, posts you view, restaurants you search for, food categories you explore, interactions (likes, comments, follows), and visited URLs before or after using our app.

3. Content You Create

We collect the content you create and share on Crunchii, including: food posts with images, restaurant reviews, comments, likes, saved favorite restaurants, your profile information, and any other content you choose to share through the app.

4. Location Information

When you use location-based features, we may collect precise or approximate location data, including GPS coordinates, to help you discover nearby restaurants, share location-tagged food posts, and provide location-based recommendations. Location data is only collected when you grant permission and use location features.
